Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

This policy explains how Safarnama Education (Sarava Parihaara) collects and uses your information when you visit safarnama.club, enrol in a programme or book a session.

Information we collect

  • Details you share when enrolling or messaging us: name, mobile number, email, city and the programme you choose.
  • Your payment reference. Payments are handled by our payment partner; we do not see or store your card, UPI PIN or bank login details.
  • Inside our learning app (when launched): which lessons you have watched, so we can show your progress. Your mobile number may appear as a watermark on course videos to protect the content.
  • Anonymous usage data through analytics and advertising tools (such as Google Analytics and the Meta Pixel), if enabled.

How we use it

  • To give you access to live classes, recordings and the learning app.
  • To send class schedules, Zoom links and updates on WhatsApp.
  • To answer your questions and improve our programmes, website and advertising.

Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only with service providers who help us run our programmes (for example, payment, video and messaging services), or when required by law.

Your choices

You can ask us to access, correct or delete your information at any time by messaging us on WhatsApp or emailing us.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version will always be on this page.
