Refund & Cancellation Policy

Last updated: November 2026

This policy applies to all Safarnama Education programmes and one-to-one sessions.

Programmes

  • If you cancel before the batch start date, message us on WhatsApp. We will refund the fee or move your seat to the next batch.
  • Once the batch has started, or once you have received access to any class or recording, the fee is non-refundable.
  • For monthly programmes, you can stop at any time before the next month's payment. Months already paid are not refunded.
  • If we cancel or postpone a batch, you can choose a full refund or a seat in the next batch.

One-to-one sessions

  • You can reschedule a session free of charge if you tell us at least 24 hours in advance.
  • Sessions missed without notice are not refunded.

Failed or duplicate payments

If money is debited for a failed or duplicate payment, it is refunded in full.

How refunds are paid

Approved refunds are made to the original payment method within 5–7 working days of approval.

Delivery

All programmes are delivered online. Zoom links and class details are shared on WhatsApp after enrolment. No physical goods are shipped.
